# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Parents and teachers manage students' study materials and exams. Students use the app to study and complete assigned exams.

## Product Purpose

Help families and teachers organize student learning and support reliable study and assessment workflows. Success means each user can understand what needs attention and act without confusion.

## Brand Personality

Clear, encouraging, dependable.

## Anti-references

Avoid cluttered interfaces and unclear exam status or actions.

## Design Principles

- Make each user's next action easy to identify.
- Keep study and assessment status clear.
- Support students without undermining teacher oversight.
- Make changes and outcomes dependable and visible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable contrast, clear labels, and keyboard-operable controls.
